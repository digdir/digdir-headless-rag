# The Veskirk glacier survey

The Veskirk glacier survey is a made-up expedition described only in this test
corpus. Its surveyors measured how far the ice front retreated each summer by
driving marker stakes into the moraine and photographing them from the same
boulder every year. Their final report concludes that the glacier lost more ice
in the decade after the survey began than in the fifty years before it, and it
recommends that the stakes stay in place for the next team.
