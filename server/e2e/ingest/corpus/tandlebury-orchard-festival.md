# The Tandlebury orchard festival

Tandlebury is an invented village whose orchard festival takes place on the
first Saturday after the apple harvest. The festival crowns a cider judge,
presses fruit on a wooden press older than anyone can remember, and ends with a
lantern walk between the rows of trees. None of this is real: the village and
its festival exist so that a retrieval test has a question with exactly one
correct document to find.
