# The Quillmoor lighthouse

The Quillmoor lighthouse stands on a granite spur at the northern end of an
invented coastline, and it exists only in this test corpus. Its keepers logged
the weather every four hours for ninety years, and the logbooks record that the
lamp was converted from paraffin to electricity in the same winter the harbour
froze solid. Visitors climb one hundred and twelve steps to reach the gallery,
where a brass plaque names every keeper who served there.
