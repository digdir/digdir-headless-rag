# Ferrovane alloy

Ferrovane is a fictional alloy invented for this test corpus. It is described as
a blend of iron and a rare earth that stays rigid in extreme cold, which is why
the stories about it are always set on polar research stations. Engineers in
those stories cast ferrovane into hinges and bearings, and complain that it is
difficult to weld because it cracks when it cools too quickly after the torch is
removed from the seam.
